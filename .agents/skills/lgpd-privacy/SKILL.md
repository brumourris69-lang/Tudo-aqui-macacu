# LGPD and Privacy Engineering

- Map data subjects, personal data, purpose, collection point, recipients, storage location, retention, and deletion path from the actual feature.
- Apply minimization, purpose limitation, access control, retention limits, and privacy-preserving defaults.
- Do not assume consent is the appropriate legal basis; flag the basis and transparency decisions for the controller and qualified counsel.
- Assess children/adolescents, sensitive data, international transfers, processors, and incident handling when relevant.
- Keep logs free of tokens and unnecessary identifiers; use aggregation or pseudonymization where practical.
- Separate technical findings from legal conclusions. Never assert full LGPD compliance without evidence and professional review.
